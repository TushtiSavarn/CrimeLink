Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the Crime Reporting System?

A: It allows people to report crimes online.

Q: Is my data secure?

A: Yes, we use security measures to protect your information.

Q: Who can use this system?

A: Anyone, including citizens, police officers, and administrators, can use the system based on their designated roles.

Q: How can I report a crime using this system?

A: You can report a crime by:
• Registering an account
• Logging in to the system
• Filling out the crime report form
• Submitting the report with necessary details

Q: How can I track the status of my complaint?

A: Once you submit a report, you will receive a report ID. You can use this ID to check the status of your complaint in the system.

Q: How do I contact support for assistance?

A: You can contact support via email at crimelink@gmail.com
