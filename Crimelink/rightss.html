Know Your Rights and Duties

Understanding Your Rights & Duties as a Citizen

Fundamental Rights

The Constitution of India guarantees certain rights to its citizens to promote equality, freedom, and justice.

  • Right to Equality: Ensures equal treatment before the law and prohibits discrimination. (Article 14-18)
  • Right to Freedom: Includes speech, expression, assembly, and movement. (Article 19-22)
  • Right Against Exploitation: Prohibits human trafficking, forced labor, and child labor. (Article 23-24)
  • Right to Freedom of Religion: Guarantees religious freedom for all citizens. (Article 25-28)
  • Right to Constitutional Remedies: Allows citizens to approach courts for rights enforcement. (Article 32-35)

Fundamental Duties

Along with rights, citizens have a set of responsibilities to uphold national integrity.

  • Respect the Constitution, National Flag, and National Anthem.
  • Promote harmony and avoid practices that harm social unity.
  • Protect the environment, including forests, lakes, and wildlife.
  • Develop a scientific temperament and humanism.
  • Safeguard public property and discourage violence.
  • Provide education to children aged 6 to 14 years.
